OWN-WORLD: Dark mode is the board: blue-black ground, flap cells, cool-white type, signal yellow. Light mode is the airport wayfinding sign: cool concrete grey, solid yellow panels, black type. Status roles: green = BOARDING / winner, red = ELIMINATED. Geist for display type; Geist Mono for flap data (codes, counts, statuses).
